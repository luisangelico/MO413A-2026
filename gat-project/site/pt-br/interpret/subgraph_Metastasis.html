Top-30 attended PPI subgraph — Metastasis

Genes the GAT attended to most when classifying Metastasis samples in the held-out test split. Node size = attention importance. Node color = Tumor − Normal differential attention (red = tumor-leaning, blue = normal-leaning). Edges are STRING physical PPIs. Drag nodes to explore; hover for details.

Attention score = sum of incoming softmax-normalized edge-attention coefficients onto this gene, averaged over the 4 GAT heads, summed over 3 layers, then averaged over correctly classified Metastasis samples. Per-layer α(i→j) lies in [0, 1] and sums to 1 over j's incoming neighbors, so the final score is roughly in [0, ~3]. Only relative differences between genes matter — a gene at 0.04 attracted ~4× the attention of one at 0.01.

Note on isolated nodes (10 of 20 here): this is the induced subgraph over the top-30 attended genes only. A gene appears isolated when its STRING PPI partners exist in the full graph but were not themselves in the top-30 for this class — the model attended to the gene, but its neighborhood was not uniformly attended.
